Dirty logistics data does not wait for a convenient moment to cause problems.

A duplicate shipment ID looks harmless in a spreadsheet. In an active supply chain, it creates confusion over what has moved, what has not, and who is responsible for what.

The ColtraDataAi Logistics and Supply Chain cleaner works through the specific failure points that appear repeatedly in real operations data:

- Duplicate shipment IDs
- Transit times recorded as zero or negative days
- Missing or inconsistent carrier codes
- Weight and unit mismatches across records
- Destination formats that do not align across the dataset

Each issue gets flagged individually. The output is a structured report your operations or planning team can work from directly - no manual hunting through rows, no guessing where the problem started.

The cleaner does not interpret what the data means for your business. It shows you precisely where the data breaks down. What happens next is your call.
