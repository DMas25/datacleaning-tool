Operational healthcare data breaks in predictable ways. Most teams don't catch it until the billing run.

Appointment logs, staff scheduling files, and billing records sit at the core of healthcare operations. They are not clinical records - but the data quality problems inside them are just as disruptive.

The ColtraDataAi Healthcare cleaner runs 35-plus validation checks built around the specific ways this data goes wrong.

That includes:

- Status code drift (appointment statuses that shift inconsistently across exports)
- Duplicate patient IDs appearing across separate records
- Impossible appointment sequences (a discharge logged before an admission)
- Missing or zero billing amounts on completed appointments

These are not edge cases. They are routine problems in operational datasets that have passed through multiple hands - reception systems, scheduling tools, finance exports - before anyone looks at them closely.

The cleaner surfaces these issues clearly and specifically. What the data contains, what is missing, what conflicts with itself. No interpretation beyond that.
