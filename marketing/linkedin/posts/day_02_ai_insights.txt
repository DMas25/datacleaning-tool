Garbage in, garbage out. It was true in 1980. It is still true now.

The rush to adopt AI tools is real. But most businesses skipping straight to deployment are missing the step that determines whether any of it works - the quality of the data going in.

An AI model trained on inconsistent supplier names, duplicate invoice references, or misformatted dates does not produce intelligent outputs. It produces confident-sounding errors at scale.

The problem is not the model. The problem is what arrives before the model ever sees it.
