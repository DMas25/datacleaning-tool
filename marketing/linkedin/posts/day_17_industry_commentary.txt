Excel is not the problem. Messy Excel is the problem.

There is a segment of the tech industry that treats spreadsheets as something to be phased out. Replace Excel with a platform. Migrate your workflows. Modernise.

For most UK SMEs, that framing misses the point entirely.

Excel works. It is flexible, familiar, and already embedded in how small businesses track invoices, reconcile accounts, and manage cash flow. The issue is not the tool - it is what arrives in the tool. Duplicate entries. Inconsistent date formats. Mixed currency symbols. Blank rows in the middle of transaction logs. Vendor names spelled four different ways.

That is a data quality problem, not a software problem.

A cleaner that demands businesses abandon their existing workflows to use it is solving the wrong thing. The more useful question is: can you clean what people actually send you, in the format they already use?
