Most data cleaning happens too late.

By the time a file gets uploaded, processed, and returned with flags, the bad data has already moved. It has been saved, referenced, sometimes acted on. Cleaning it becomes a correction exercise rather than a prevention one.

The shift happening now is validation at the point of entry.

Forms that flag formatting issues as someone types. APIs that check records on ingestion before they touch a database. Pipelines that reject malformed data rather than absorbing it and passing the problem downstream.

This is where automated cleaning is heading - from scheduled batch jobs to continuous, embedded validation that catches issues the moment data moves.

The ColtraDataAi Enterprise API is built for exactly this: run domain-specific validation checks against your data in transit, not after it has settled somewhere you have to unpick later.

Finance records, logistics data, import/export entries, inventory feeds - each checked against the rules that matter for that domain, at the point your pipeline needs it.
