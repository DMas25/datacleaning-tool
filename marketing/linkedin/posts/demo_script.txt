ColtraDataAi - Live Demo Script
Version 1.0 | 2026-08-14
===============================================================
FORMAT NOTES
  - VOICEOVER: Read this aloud exactly as written when recording.
    Aim for a calm, direct delivery. No filler. No enthusiasm performance.
  - [LIVE]: Delivery guidance for a real call - adapt freely, this is
    a prompt not a constraint.
  - [SCREEN]: What to have visible or what to click at that moment.
  - Total runtime (recording): approx. 2.5 minutes at a measured pace.
  - For a live sales call, expand each section with questions and pauses.
    Budget 5-8 minutes including prospect interaction.

DOMAIN PLACEHOLDER
  Replace [DOMAIN] with one of the following before recording or presenting.
  Pair it with the [KEY FINDING] for that domain (see substitution table below).

  Domain              | [DOMAIN] label              | [KEY FINDING]
  --------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------------
  Finance             | Finance and Accounting      | invalid VAT codes and a trial balance that does not close
  Logistics           | Logistics and Supply Chain  | delivery dates recorded before dispatch dates
  Trade               | Import/Export and Trade     | negative declared values and non-standard HS codes
  Retail              | Retail and Inventory        | items where cost price exceeds selling price
  SME                 | SME and Small Business      | overdue invoices and invalid UK VAT numbers
  Consultants         | Consultants and Services    | timesheet entries exceeding 24 hours in a single day
  Healthcare          | Healthcare Operations       | NHS numbers failing Mod 11 validation
  Hospitality         | Hospitality                 | check-out dates recorded before check-in
  Clinical            | Clinical Research           | the same researcher appearing under multiple name variants

PART 1 - COLD OPEN (0:00 - 0:20)
===============================================================

[SCREEN: ColtraDataAi landing page or the Live Demo page loading]

VOICEOVER:
Most data quality problems are invisible until they have already caused a
problem. A report built on bad data does not tell you it is wrong. It just
gives you the wrong answer.

[LIVE: "Before I show you the tool - does that sound familiar in your work?
What does bad data actually cost you in a typical month?" - wait for the
answer. This is the most important moment of the call.]

===============================================================
PART 2 - THE PROBLEM SETUP (0:20 - 0:50)
===============================================================

[SCREEN: Stay on landing page or navigate to the demo tab for [DOMAIN]]

VOICEOVER:
The [DOMAIN] cleaner inside ColtraDataAi is built for exactly this. It
runs a structured set of domain-specific validation checks against your
data before it reaches anyone who needs to rely on it.

Not generic checks. Checks that were written specifically for [DOMAIN]
data - the inconsistencies and errors that show up repeatedly in that
kind of dataset and that a spreadsheet formula will not catch.

[LIVE: "What does your current process look like for checking a new file
before it goes into your system?" - this surfaces manual pain, positions
the product.]

===============================================================
PART 3 - RAW DATA (0:50 - 1:15)
===============================================================

[SCREEN: Navigate to app.coltradata.com/Live_Demo if not already there.
Click the [DOMAIN] tab. Let the raw data table load and sit on screen
for a moment before speaking.]

VOICEOVER:
This is a sample dataset that represents what we typically see from a
[DOMAIN] export. It looks fine at a glance. Columns are populated. There
is data in every row.

But it contains [KEY FINDING], along with a number of other issues that
are easy to miss when you are reviewing a file manually.

[LIVE: "I will let you read the data for a second. What do you notice?"
- give them time. They rarely spot everything. That contrast lands harder
when the results appear.]

===============================================================
PART 4 - RUN THE DEMO (1:15 - 1:25)
===============================================================

[SCREEN: Click the "Run Demo - See what ColtraDataAi finds" button.
Let the spinner run. Do not talk over this moment - let the silence work.]

VOICEOVER:
[SAY NOTHING during the spinner. Let the pause build the anticipation.]

[LIVE: If they ask "how long does it take?" - "On a real file, a few
seconds per thousand rows. On this sample it is near-instant."]

===============================================================
PART 5 - THE FINDINGS (1:25 - 2:00)
===============================================================

[SCREEN: The results section appears. Keep the findings list visible.
Point at specific rows in the issues list as you describe them.]

VOICEOVER:
These are the findings. Each line is a specific category of issue - the
type, a plain-English description, and the count of affected rows.

The ones flagged in amber are problems. The ones in green passed the check.

What you are looking at is a structured audit of the dataset. It tells
you exactly what the data contains and where the problems are - without
interpretation, without advisory language, and without asking you to
trust a human's judgment about what they reviewed and what they missed.

[LIVE: Walk through the flagged findings one by one, reading the type
and count aloud. Then ask: "If that came into your system unflagged -
what would happen downstream?" Let them answer. The answer is usually the
sale.]

===============================================================
PART 6 - THE CLEANED DATA (2:00 - 2:20)
===============================================================

[SCREEN: Scroll down to the "Cleaned data - after processing" table.
Point out changes in key columns - standardised values, removed duplicates.]

VOICEOVER:
And this is the cleaned version. Duplicates removed. Standardised values
where the cleaner could normalise them automatically. And where it could
not fix something without a human decision, it has flagged it rather than
guessing.

You get the cleaned dataset, a flagged row report, and a plain-language
summary of what changed. All three ready to hand straight to whoever
needs them.

[LIVE: "Does your current process produce anything like this as an output -
or is it more of a word-of-mouth 'I checked it' kind of handoff?"]

===============================================================
CLOSING NOTES FOR LIVE CALLS
===============================================================

If they ask "how is this different from just writing formulas in Excel?":
  "Excel formulas check what you tell them to check. This checks what
   we know goes wrong in [DOMAIN] data - based on the patterns that
   cause problems repeatedly. You do not have to build the logic. It is
   already there."

If they ask "what about data security / does my data leave my system?":
  "The file is processed in memory. It is never written to disk. We do
   not store, share, or retain any file content. The output exists in
   your session only."

If they ask "can we try it with our actual data?":
  "Yes - that is exactly what the free plan is for. Sign up, upload a
   real file, and you will see findings within seconds. You do not need
   to commit to anything until you have seen it on your own data."

If they go quiet after the findings reveal:
  Do not fill the silence. Let them sit with what they just saw.
  That pause is where the value lands.
