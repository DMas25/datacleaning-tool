Manual upload workflows break at scale.

When your team is processing thousands of records across multiple domains, clicking through a UI is not a pipeline. It is a bottleneck.

The ColtraDataAi Enterprise API exposes all eight domain cleaners through a single REST endpoint: POST /v1/clean/{domain}. Send CSV or JSON. Get back a cleaned dataset and a structured findings object that flags exactly what was wrong with the incoming data.

Every call is authenticated via Bearer token. Usage is logged per request. Swagger docs are included so your developers know the schema.

The eight domains available via API: Finance & Accounting, Logistics & Supply Chain, Retail & Inventory, Import/Export & Trade, Healthcare (Operational), Consultants & Professional Services, SME & Small Business, and Hospitality & Accommodation.

Built on FastAPI. Deployed at coltradata-api.onrender.com.

If you are building a data pipeline and need domain-specific validation at the ingestion layer, integrate this endpoint.
