Most analysts aren't doing analysis. They're cleaning spreadsheets.

Industry surveys put data preparation time at 60 to 80% of a data professional's working week. That's not a skills problem. That's an infrastructure problem.

Businesses spend on dashboards. They spend on BI tools. They invest in analysts. Then they feed all of it with data that hasn't been validated, standardised, or checked for consistency.

The output looks polished. The input is chaos.

The question most teams ask is: "What does our data show us?"

The question they should be asking first is: "Is our data in a state where it can show anything reliable?"

Those are very different questions. And skipping the second one doesn't make the first one easier - it just makes the answer less trustworthy.

Data cleaning isn't a junior task to be skipped. It's the foundation everything else sits on.
