Frequently asked questions

Can I try ColtraDataAi before paying?
Yes — the Free plan lets you run up to 3 cleaning sessions per month on datasets up to 5,000 rows. No credit card required. Just enter your email at app.coltradata.com to get started instantly.
How does billing work?
Subscriptions are billed monthly via Lemon Squeezy, our payment processor. You'll receive a receipt immediately after payment. Cancel anytime from your Lemon Squeezy billing portal — no questions asked.
How does my plan activate after purchase?
After checkout, return to app.coltradata.com and sign in with the same email you used at purchase. Your paid plan is detected automatically — no licence key entry needed.
What file types are supported?
ColtraDataAi supports CSV and Excel (.xlsx / .xls) files. Upload any messy spreadsheet and the platform handles missing values, duplicates, and mixed data types automatically.
Is my data secure?
Your data is processed securely and never permanently stored or shared with third parties. ColtraDataAi is GDPR-compliant and built for professional use. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes. To upgrade, simply purchase the new plan via the checkout link above — your subscription will update automatically. To cancel or get help, email support@coltradata.com.
How does Enterprise pricing work?
Enterprise starts from £999/month and is tailored to your organisation's scale, SLA requirements, and data volume. Contact us at sales@coltradata.com to discuss your requirements and get a custom quote.
What is the Enterprise API?
The Enterprise API is a standalone developer product that exposes all 8 ColtraDataAi domain cleaners as REST endpoints. At £499/month, it's designed for teams who want to embed data cleaning directly into their own systems or pipelines. Your API key is delivered automatically on purchase. Full Swagger docs available at coltradata-api.onrender.com/docs.
