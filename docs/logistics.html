The Challenge

Sound Familiar?

Logistics data comes from multiple sources and depots, and it rarely arrives consistently formatted or complete.

🏭

Data from Multiple Depots in Different Formats

Each depot, carrier, or system exports data slightly differently. Consolidating it manually creates new inconsistencies and introduces errors that flow into planning and billing.

📦

Shipment Records with Missing or Wrong Values

Blank delivery dates, incorrect weight entries, missing consignment references: errors in the source data are costly when they feed into route planning, billing, or performance reporting.

🏢

Supplier Data Inconsistently Formatted

Supplier names, codes, and contact details entered differently across records make supplier management reporting unreliable and time-consuming to reconcile before each reporting cycle.

📉

No Single Source of Truth Across Operations

Without clean, consolidated data, operational performance reporting is always an estimate rather than a fact, making it harder to identify inefficiencies and harder to justify decisions.
