The Challenge

Sound Familiar?

Finance teams know their numbers — but getting the data into a usable state takes longer than it should.

📅

Month-End Reporting Takes Too Long

Cleaning and preparing data from multiple systems before reporting can even begin eats days every period. By the time reports are ready, the window for management action has already passed.

🔀

Data Arrives Inconsistently from Multiple Systems

ERP exports, bank feeds, payroll files, supplier data — each arrives in a different format. Reconciling them manually is time-consuming and introduces new errors at every step.

🎫

Waiting on IT for Simple Reports

A straightforward report request sits in an IT backlog for weeks. Finance should never need a developer to produce a management summary or a board-pack data extract.

📧

Version Control Chaos in Shared Spreadsheets

Data passed around by email, re-cleaned differently by different people, overwritten without a record. No single source of truth and no audit trail when figures are questioned.
