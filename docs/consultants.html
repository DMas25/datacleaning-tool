The Challenge

Sound Familiar?

Every engagement starts with the same problem: data that needs cleaning before any real analysis can begin.

📁

Data Always Needs Cleaning First

Before you can analyse anything, you need to fix the data. Inconsistent formatting, duplicates, missing values — the cleaning work eats into hours you should be billing for insights.

No Time to Clean While Billing Hours

Clients pay for your expertise and judgement, not for you to tidy spreadsheets. Every hour spent cleaning is an hour not spent on the strategic work that justifies your rate.

Presenting Unpolished Data Looks Unprofessional

Raw data handed to clients or stakeholders sends the wrong signal. You need clean, visualised, clearly structured output — fast — to present with the authority your clients expect.

🔄

Data Quality Issues Corrupt Your Analysis

Dirty data produces unreliable results. Chasing outliers caused by formatting errors wastes time and risks undermining conclusions that your reputation depends on.
