The Challenge

Sound Familiar?

Clinical and administrative data in healthcare comes from multiple systems, and it is rarely consistent, complete, or submission-ready.

🧑‍⚕️

Patient Records Inconsistent Across Systems

The same patient recorded with three different date-of-birth formats, two NHS number variations, and inconsistent address entries across your EPR, referral system, and reporting extract; reconciling them manually is slow and error-prone.

📋

Missing Clinical Codes Before National Submissions

Blank ICD-10 diagnosis codes, missing OPCS procedure entries, incomplete HRG groupings: gaps that look minor in a spreadsheet become rejection notices when the data reaches NHS Digital or your ICB.

👥

Duplicate Patient Entries Across Wards and Departments

Patients entered multiple times under slightly different names, with different NHS numbers or date formats: duplicates that distort episode counts, inflate activity data, and undermine the accuracy of performance reporting.

📉

Waiting List Data with Gaps That Distort Performance

Missing referral dates, blank pathway stages, incomplete RTT status fields: errors in waiting list data produce misleading performance dashboards and expose the organisation to scrutiny during CQC visits and commissioner reviews.
