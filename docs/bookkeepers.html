The Challenge

Sound Familiar?

The data that arrives from small business clients is rarely ready to work with. These are the problems ColtraDataAi was built to solve.

📂

Messy Client Exports

Every client hands over data differently. Inconsistent date formats, mixed currency symbols, broken column headers: each file needs manual work before you can open it in your accounting software.

🔍

Errors of Omission & Commission

Missing transactions, duplicated entries, and miscategorised records buried in hundreds of rows. Finding them by eye takes hours you're not billing.

📋

Incomplete Records

Small businesses capture transactions inconsistently. Gaps in records only surface once you're deep into the file, costing time you didn't plan for and conversations you didn't want to have.

⚠️

No Cleaning Audit Trail

Manually cleaning data in Excel leaves no record of what was changed, when, or why. That's a risk when a client disputes a figure or when the books are reviewed.
