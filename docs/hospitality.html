The Challenge

Sound Familiar?

Hospitality data comes from multiple systems and channels and rarely arrives in a usable state. These are the problems ColtraDataAi was built to solve.

🏨

PMS Exports Are Inconsistent

Booking records exported from your property management system arrive with mismatched date formats, inconsistent room type labels, duplicate reservation IDs, and guest name formats that change between exports.

🍽️

F&B Inventory Data Conflicts Across Outlets

Different kitchens, bars, and restaurants use different item codes, unit descriptions, and supplier naming conventions. Consolidated stock reporting across outlets requires hours of manual reconciliation.

📅

Duplicate Reservations From Multiple Channels

Direct bookings, OTA imports, and walk-in records all end up in the same export, creating duplicate entries that distort occupancy figures, revenue reports, and rate analysis.

👤

Payroll and Rota Data Full of Errors

Rota spreadsheets with missing employee IDs, inconsistent shift codes, and date format mismatches between scheduling tools and payroll software create reconciliation headaches every pay period.
